A NOTE FROM YOUR LOVER

Dear,
Sodiq.

Not a speech. Not a special occasion checklist. Just the words I want you to have, in writing.

For all our ordinary days.

There are things about you I notice that I probably don't say enough.

I notice the effort you put into the people and things you love. The way you show up, even on days that ask more of you than they should. The way you keep becoming, quietly and steadily, the man you want to be.

And I love the beautifully ordinary parts of life with you just as much as the big moments. The laughter over absolutely nothing. The conversations that go on longer than we meant them to. The comfort of simply knowing you're there.

I hope this little collection reminds you that you are seen, appreciated, prayed for, and so very loved.

There is so much more I could say. Perhaps that's why I made the rest of this website.

Always, your sweetheart ♡
